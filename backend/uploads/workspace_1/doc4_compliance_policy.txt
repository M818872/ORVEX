NOVACORE TECHNOLOGIES GOVERNANCE POLICY
Policy Reference: OPS-GOV-001
Title: Production Product Launch Governance & Customer Notice Requirements
Version: 4.1
Effective Date: January 1, 2026
Policy Authority: Vice President of Operations & Compliance Oversight Committee

1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY:
This policy establishes mandatory operational governance standards for all production software releases and major platform announcements at NovaCore Technologies, specifically governing the NovaCore Edge Platform.

2. MANDATORY ADVANCE CUSTOMER NOTICE:
Section 2.1 — Notice Window Requirement:
Any production launch date that has been publicly announced or externally committed to enterprise clients requires a MINIMUM OF 14 CALENDAR DAYS advance notification prior to the scheduled launch date.
- Original launch date: October 15, 2026.
- Mandatory notice cutoff date: October 1, 2026 (Satisfied).

Section 2.2 — Schedule Modification Notice Rules:
If a scheduled production launch is postponed or rescheduled after external notification:
(a) Written formal notice must be sent to all contracted enterprise accounts at least 7 calendar days before any new launch date.
(b) A launch date change cannot be announced without explicit written authorization from the VP of Operations (Sarah Chen).

3. GOVERNANCE APPROVAL GATES:
Prior to production traffic cutover, the following mandatory sign-offs must be logged in the NovaCore audit system:
- Gate G-1: Engineering Lead architectural sign-off (Daniel Okafor)
- Gate G-2: QA Director verification sign-off (Tom Hendricks)
- Gate G-3: Finance Director budget and revenue reconciliation sign-off (Marcus Webb)
- Gate G-4: Executive VP Operations final deployment authorization (Sarah Chen)

4. POLICY EXCEPTION CLAUSE:
In the event of an unforeseen external vendor failure or upstream dependency outage, the VP of Operations possesses sole discretion to invoke an Emergency Policy Exception (Section 4.3), permitting a schedule realignment provided all enterprise accounts receive personalized account manager briefings within 24 hours of decision confirmation.
