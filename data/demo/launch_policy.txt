1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

1.1 PURPOSE

This policy establishes the governance requirements for planning, approving, communicating, and executing product launch date changes at NovaCore Technologies. It ensures that launch date changes are managed in a controlled, auditable, and stakeholder-aware manner, minimizing business risk and protecting customer commitments.

1.2 SCOPE

This policy applies to:
  - All production software releases
  - All enterprise product launches
  - Any externally communicated launch, release, or delivery date
  - All changes to previously announced or internally committed launch dates
  - All NovaCore employees, contractors, and third-party partners involved in launch planning

1.3 EXCLUSIONS

This policy does not apply to:
  - Internal development milestones not communicated externally
  - Patch releases or security hotfixes with explicit emergency classification
  - Beta or preview releases explicitly labeled as non-committal

2. CORE LAUNCH DATE CHANGE REQUIREMENTS

2.1 MINIMUM NOTICE REQUIREMENT

Any change to a production launch date that has been formally communicated to customers, partners, or external stakeholders requires a MINIMUM OF 14 CALENDAR DAYS notice prior to the original confirmed date.

EXAMPLE: If the original launch date is October 1, 2026, a date change notification must be issued no later than September 17, 2026, to satisfy the 14-day minimum notice requirement.

Failure to provide 14 days notice constitutes a policy breach and requires post-event reporting to the Executive Leadership Committee.

2.2 OPERATIONS APPROVAL REQUIREMENT

ANY change to a production launch date — regardless of the notice period available — requires explicit written approval from the VP of Operations or designee before:
  (a) The change is communicated to external parties
  (b) The new date is treated as an internal commitment
  (c) Any project plan, JIRA, or scheduling system is updated to reflect the new date as confirmed

Operations approval must be documented in writing (email or formal approval record) and referenced in the project record.

2.3 DUAL REQUIREMENT CLAUSE

Both conditions (14-day notice AND Operations approval) are mandatory. Satisfying only one condition does not constitute compliance with this policy.

EXCEPTION CLAUSE: If the notice period cannot be satisfied due to circumstances outside NovaCore's control (e.g., critical vendor failure, security incident, regulatory requirement), Operations approval alone may authorize a policy exception. The exception must be documented in writing and reported to the Executive Leadership Committee within 3 business days.

3. APPROVAL PROCESS

3.1 STANDARD APPROVAL PROCESS

Step 1 — Impact Assessment
  The Project Manager must prepare a Launch Date Change Impact Assessment covering:
    - Reason for proposed date change
    - Affected customers and contracts
    - Revenue impact (if any)
    - Technical justification
    - Risk assessment
    - Alternative date rationale

Step 2 — Stakeholder Notification
  The following stakeholders must be notified before Operations approval is sought:
    - Finance Director (budget/revenue impact)
    - Product Director (customer commitments)
    - Engineering Lead (technical feasibility)
    - Legal (contractual obligations, if applicable)

Step 3 — Operations Review
  VP Operations reviews the impact assessment and stakeholder input.
  Decision window: 2 business days for standard review.

Step 4 — Formal Approval
  VP Operations issues written approval or rejection with reasons.

Step 5 — Communication
  Post-approval, Communications / Product team issues customer notification within 24 hours.

Step 6 — Audit Record
  Project Manager updates the project record and audit trail with the approved change.

3.2 EMERGENCY APPROVAL PROCESS

For critical situations where a 2-business-day review is not feasible:
  - Engineering Lead or VP Operations may invoke Emergency Protocol
  - Emergency approval requires verbal authorization from VP Operations
  - Written ratification required within 24 hours
  - Automatic escalation to Executive Leadership Committee within 3 business days

4. CUSTOMER COMMUNICATION REQUIREMENTS

4.1 NOTIFICATION CONTENT

All external launch date change notifications must include:
  - Original committed date
  - New proposed date
  - Reason for change (summary, non-technical)
  - Customer impact assessment
  - NovaCore point of contact for questions
  - Any contractual implications

4.2 NOTIFICATION CHANNELS

Tier 1 Enterprise Customers (annual contract value > $500K): Direct call from VP/Director + written notice
Tier 2 Enterprise Customers: Written notice (email from Account Manager)
General Customers: Platform announcement + email notification

4.3 NO PRE-APPROVAL COMMUNICATION

Under no circumstances may any employee, contractor, or partner communicate a new launch date to external parties before Operations approval has been granted. Violation of this requirement will be treated as a policy breach and subject to HR review.

5. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

5.1 POLICY BREACH

Failure to comply with this policy may result in:
  - Formal incident report to Executive Leadership Committee
  - HR review for responsible parties
  - Customer escalation management by VP Operations
  - Remediation plan required within 5 business days

5.2 AUDIT TRAIL

All launch date changes must be recorded in the Project Management system with:
  - Date of change decision
  - Approving authority
  - Approval reference number
  - Customer notification date and method

5.3 REPORTING

VP Operations will report launch date change incidents (including policy exceptions) to the Executive Leadership Committee on a quarterly basis.
