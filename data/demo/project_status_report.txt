2. EXECUTIVE STATUS SUMMARY

The Apex Platform is feature-complete across all primary modules. Core product, analytics, and user management have passed UAT. However, the project is currently rated AMBER due to a critical external dependency: the Meridian Payments API integration is blocked pending vendor delivery of a corrected API specification. This dependency directly affects the enterprise billing module, which is required by approximately 40% of contracted enterprise customers.

A launch date change from October 1 to October 15 was proposed in today's launch review meeting. This change is not yet approved and is pending Operations governance review and Meridian vendor confirmation.
